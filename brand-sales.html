Brand Sales

Appliance units by brand, straight from the ePASS serial feed, counted two ways side by side: Written = units on tickets the sales team started in the window (invoice start date; finished tickets plus what is still open on the book), which tracks selling behavior as the mix shifts; Delivered = units on tickets finished in the window, with revenue net of linked ePASS discounts, the unit's own cost and the margin. Brands come from the ePASS Model master; returned units are shown apart and left out of both counts. Click a brand for its models, and a model for the units behind it (delivered serials and open, undelivered lines). Anything the master hasn't covered lands in its own bucket at the bottom so nothing quietly disappears.
