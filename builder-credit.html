Terms of Sale

Approved accounts are sold on net thirty (30) day terms from date of invoice. Balances outstanding beyond forty-five (45) days from the invoice date are subject to a service charge of one and one-half percent (1.5%) per month (18% annually), or the maximum rate permitted by Texas law, whichever is less. Past due balances or purchases beyond the approved credit limit may result in suspension of deliveries, installations, scheduling, or credit privileges until the account is brought current and within limit. In the event collection efforts become necessary, the applicant agrees to pay all costs of collection, including reasonable attorney fees and court costs, and agrees that venue for any action arising under this agreement lies in Hays County, Texas. Claims for damage, shortage, or product defect must be submitted in writing within three (3) business days of delivery. Returns and cancellations of special-order, custom, or build-to-order products are subject to manufacturer approval and may be denied or subject to restocking fees; authorized returns of standard assortment merchandise are subject to a twenty-five percent (25%) restocking fee.

The summary above is part of our complete Builder & Trade Account Credit Terms (PDF), which apply to all approved accounts.
