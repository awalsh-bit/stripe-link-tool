Catalog Check — NetSuite vs ePASS

NetSuite is the source of truth for brand, supplier and the merchandise hierarchy; ePASS is where the models are sold and stocked. This compares the two for every model ePASS knows (model master, open orders, on-hand stock) against the NetSuite item export last uploaded on the Ordering Report → NetSuite items & suppliers (or ePASS Uploads → NetSuite Item Catalog — either one fills both tables). Nothing is changed here — fix the item in NetSuite, re-export, re-import, refresh. Agility already speaks NetSuite wherever it can: brand, supplier and merch class come from NetSuite for every model it has, and a model in the “In ePASS, not in NetSuite” tile is exactly one that is still being described with ePASS's own words. Supplier aliases for ePASS codes are learned from the models and can be overridden on the Ordering Report → Settings page.
