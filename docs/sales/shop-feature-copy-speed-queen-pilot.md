# Shop product pages: Speed Queen feature copy (pilot)

*Draft for a tone review, 11/17. Nothing here is on the shop yet.*

Five features per model, each with a line of Wilson's own copy. The 24 Speed Queen models with product details fall into 11 families; finish, fuel and door-hinge versions share one set of copy.

**How it was written.** The facts come from Speed Queen's product pages (what each feature is, the figures, the warranty). The wording is ours. Feature names such as Perfect Wash and Pet Plus are Speed Queen's and are used as names. Where Speed Queen makes a claim rather than states a fact, the line says so ("helps kill bacteria", "Speed Queen builds this washer for 25 years").

**What I need from you.** Is the tone right? Are these the right five for each model? Each family lists what could be swapped in.

## Things to check before any of this goes live

**The shop's current labels disagree with Speed Queen's pages in five places.**

| Model | The shop says | Speed Queen's page says |
|---|---|---|
| TC5003BN | 840 RPM Max Spin Speed | 710 rpm |
| TC5003BN | 6 Cycles and 4 Preset Cycles (both listed) | 6 preset cycles |
| DR7004 (all four) | Adaptive Cool-Down Technology | Commercial Cool-Down Technology |
| FF7011 / FF7012 | Pet Plus Cycle | Pet Plus Flea Cycle |
| FF7011 / FF7012 | Allergy Rinse | not on the page I read |

**Left out because I could not confirm it.**

- **Delayed Lid-Lock (TR7, TR5, TR3).** Speed Queen leads with it, but its pages do not say when the lid locks. It should probably be one of the five once that is confirmed from the user guide.
- **ENERGY STAR on DC5 and DF7.** The page I could read for each covers one fuel type only (DC5 gas, DF7 electric), so the line leaves it out for both.
- **What "Oxi" is.** Speed Queen's pages do not name the additive, so the line does not either.

**Read from a sister model's page.** The pages for DR7004WE, DC5004WE and SF7008WE would not load. Their facts come from DR7004WG, DC5004WG and SF7008BE, which are the same machines in another fuel or finish.

**Claims worded with care.**

- *Sanitize.* Speed Queen says these cycles help kill bacteria and gives no percentage or certification. The copy says "helps" and no more.
- *The flea cycle.* Speed Queen says "designed and tested to help fight", so the copy does not promise more.
- *The TC5's figures* (the 210-degree stroke, sixty strokes a minute, deep fill, "about half an hour") come from Speed Queen's brand page and its TC5 article, not the model's own page, which names the features without figures.
- *The warranty* is stated as parts and labor. One page notes that the full warranty needs the serial number registered.

**Trademark marks.** Speed Queen writes Perfect Wash™, Classic Clean™ and Pet Plus™. The draft leaves the marks off; they can be added on first mention if you prefer.
