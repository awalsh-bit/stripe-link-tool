How Wilson maintenance works — and what to expect from it.

Everything on this page exists so that nothing about your plan, your visits, or your reports is ever a surprise. It covers how we think about maintenance, what a visit does and deliberately does not do, what Wilson Refrigeration Guardian can and cannot promise, the questions we hear most, and the terms the whole service runs on.

Our guiding principles

Our play is to be observant.

We measure, and we remember.

Every visit runs the same standardized checks on the same appliance, the same way, year after year. One reading is an opinion; the same reading trending across three years is knowledge. Your reports are built from that history, not from a glance.

Honesty over drama.

A health score never drops because an appliance is a certain brand, looks worn, or arrived dirty. Condition is what we measured after the work; the age of the machine is shown beside it, in the open, never folded in silently.

We are not here to sell you a replacement.

Our job is to keep what you own running and to tell you the truth about it. When something is genuinely near the end, the data will say so on its own — and the decision stays yours.

Intervene when the data says to.

We do not take healthy machines apart to look busy. We watch closely, and when a trend turns — a drain slowing, a condenser working harder each year — we address it then, deliberately, with a quote you approve first.

What a maintenance visit is — and is not

Observant maintenance, not annual surgery.

A Wilson maintenance visit is a disciplined pass over every enrolled appliance: cleaning of the accessible service points, standardized performance tests, safety checks, filter replacement where covered, and a health report with photographs and measured readings you can compare year over year.

It is not a teardown or a deep-clean service. Appliances are not built to be disassembled once or twice a year, every year — a dryer, for example, is not meant to be taken apart annually to chase lint into the base. Routine disassembly creates more problems than it prevents: fatigued fasteners, disturbed seals, stressed connectors. So we clean and inspect what the manufacturer made accessible, we measure performance from the outside in, and we watch the trend.

When observation says more is needed — lint accumulation actually measurable at the vent, a condenser that cleaning no longer restores, growth inside an icemaker beyond a sanitize — we tell you, we quote the deeper work separately, and we do it with your approval. That keeps teardowns rare, deliberate, and justified by data rather than by a calendar.

And a plain truth up front: well-maintained components can still fail without warning. A fan motor that tested healthy in March can fail in April — that is the nature of machines, and no inspection anywhere can promise otherwise. What maintenance buys you is fewer preventable failures, early sight of the slow ones, and a complete record when something does go wrong. What it cannot buy is a guarantee that nothing ever breaks.

Wilson Refrigeration Guardian

What Guardian does — and what it cannot promise.

Guardian places professional temperature sensors in your refrigeration — in the compartments you choose — and watches them around the clock. When a compartment runs outside its safe range and does not recover the way a healthy unit should, Wilson is alerted and treats it with dispatch priority: our goal is to be working on the problem before a quiet failure becomes a spoiled kitchen.

What Guardian is: continuous monitoring, honest thresholds tuned to how real refrigeration behaves (a defrost cycle or a door left open for a party is not an emergency), a priority response when something is genuinely wrong, and the same reading history feeding your maintenance reports.

What Guardian is not: it is not a promise that nothing will fail, and it is not insurance. Guardian shortens the time between a problem starting and Wilson knowing about it — it cannot prevent a compressor from failing, and it cannot see anything while it has no power or no internet connection. If your home's Wi-Fi or power goes down, the sensors cannot report; we alert on prolonged silence too, but a sensor that cannot reach us is a sensor that cannot warn us.

Guardian complements maintenance rather than replacing it — the sensors watch temperature; the technician's hands and instruments still do what no sensor can.

Frequently asked questions
My appliance had a healthy report and failed a month later. How?

Because machines can do that, and we would rather tell you so up front than pretend otherwise. A health check is a truthful snapshot plus a trend — it catches wear that develops gradually, which is most of it. Sudden component deaths (a fan motor, a control board) can happen with no measurable warning at all. Your report history still matters after a failure: it shows the rest of the machine's condition and informs whether a repair is worth it.

Why didn't the technician take my dryer apart?

Because it did not need it, and routine disassembly does more harm than good. We clean and inspect everything the manufacturer made accessible and measure the vent system's actual performance. If those measurements say lint is becoming a restriction, we will tell you and quote the deeper cleaning then — as its own job, done properly.

Does my appliance's score drop just because it is old?

No. The condition score is built from what the technician measured on this visit. Age is tracked and shown beside it as "life used," openly — an immaculate fourteen-year-old refrigerator will show excellent condition and honest mileage, side by side, not a mysteriously low single number.

Will you pressure me to replace things?

No. Nothing in how we score or pay our people rewards a replacement recommendation. When repair costs genuinely stop making sense against a machine's remaining life, your reports will have been showing the slide for years, and we will lay out the options with the data on the table.

How does filter service pricing work if you never asked for my model number?

Deliberately simply: registration quotes filter service at our standard per-filter rate so you are not stuck hunting for a model number just to sign up. Before your first charge, our office verifies the exact filter your equipment takes, and if it differs from the standard rate we adjust the price and tell you. You always end up paying for the filter you actually have.

What happens when Guardian raises an alert?

The reading is judged against thresholds built for how refrigeration really behaves — brief warm-ups from defrost cycles and door openings are expected and ignored. A compartment that is genuinely out of range and failing to recover alerts Wilson directly, and we respond with dispatch priority: contact first, truck when it is warranted.

What if my internet or power goes out — is Guardian still watching?

No — and we will not pretend it is. Sensors need power and your home network to report. If a sensor goes silent for an extended period we treat the silence itself as an alert and check on it, but a sensor that cannot reach us cannot warn us. Guardian is monitoring, not magic.

Can I add or remove appliances mid-year?

Yes, even during a visit. If you add an appliance and the technician services it on the spot, that appliance is charged at its full plan price — you are getting the full year of service starting that day. Coverage reductions and plan changes are prorated against the time left in your plan year, take effect after our office confirms them, and your renewal simply bills the new annual.

What is a follow-up quote?

When a technician finds something during maintenance that needs real repair work, they flag it and build a quote after the visit. It reaches you through our estimate-approval system by email — you approve or decline there, and nothing gets repaired without your yes.

Is the deep cleaning or teardown work included in my plan?

No — by design. Deep cleans, teardowns, duct and blower cleaning, and repair work are quoted separately when observations justify them. Keeping them out of the plan is what keeps the plan honest: you pay for disciplined maintenance every year, and for surgery only when surgery is actually indicated.

Terms & conditions

Plan terms, in plain language.Working draft

This draft states the working terms of Wilson maintenance plans and the Guardian monitoring service. It is written to be read, not to hide things — and it will be reviewed by counsel before it becomes the signed agreement.

1. What the service is

Wilson maintenance plans provide scheduled preventive maintenance visits: cleaning of accessible service points, standardized performance testing, safety checks, covered filter replacement, and written health reports for each enrolled appliance. The service is observational and preventive in nature. It does not include disassembly beyond manufacturer-intended access, deep cleaning, restoration, or repair work, all of which are quoted separately and performed only with your approval.

2. No guarantee against failure

Maintenance reduces preventable failures and surfaces gradual decline early. It does not and cannot guarantee that any appliance or component will not fail. Mechanical and electrical components can fail suddenly and without measurable warning, including shortly after a satisfactory inspection. A health score is a truthful record of measured condition at a point in time — it is not a warranty, a prediction, or an insurance product.

3. Guardian monitoring

Guardian provides continuous temperature monitoring of enrolled refrigeration compartments and a priority response when readings breach the service's alert rules. Monitoring depends on sensor power and your home internet connection; Wilson cannot receive readings from a sensor that is offline, and extended sensor silence is itself treated as an alert condition. Guardian does not prevent equipment failure and is not a food-spoilage insurance policy; our commitment is monitoring, honest alerting, and priority dispatch, and our liability for spoilage or consequential loss is limited accordingly (see section 7).

4. Billing, changes, and proration

Plans bill on the schedule stated in your enrollment and quote. Appliances added mid-term and serviced at that visit are charged at their full plan price, since the full service year begins immediately. Other mid-term changes — coverage reductions, plan-level changes, add-on adjustments — are prorated to the time remaining in the plan year and take effect once confirmed by our office. At renewal, the plan bills its then-current annual amount on the normal schedule. Filter service is priced at our standard per-filter rate at registration and verified against your actual equipment before your first charge, with any difference adjusted and communicated.

5. Access, safety, and site conditions

You agree to provide reasonable access to enrolled equipment at scheduled visits. Wilson technicians will not perform work they judge unsafe, that requires moving built-in equipment beyond its service clearances, or that would require disassembly outside manufacturer-intended access; any such condition is documented in your report instead.

6. Changes and cancellation

You may amend the plan (add or remove appliances, change add-ons) at any time, including in the field during a visit; changes to money or scope are confirmed by our office. You may cancel the plan at renewal. Mid-term cancellation and refund handling will be finalized with counsel review; our working intent is that completed visits are earned and the unused remainder of a prepaid year is handled fairly and stated clearly before you sign.

7. Limitation of liability

Wilson's responsibility under a maintenance plan is to perform the described services with professional care. Except where the law says otherwise, Wilson is not liable for equipment failure occurring despite maintenance, for spoilage or other consequential losses arising from equipment failure or from monitoring interruptions outside Wilson's control (including power and internet outages), or for conditions that could only have been found through disassembly the service deliberately does not perform. Nothing in these terms limits liability that cannot lawfully be limited.

8. Your data

Visit records, photographs, measured readings, and Guardian temperature data for your home are used to deliver the service: building your reports, judging alerts, and improving the accuracy of the service's thresholds. We do not sell your data. Photographs taken during visits document the work and stay in your service record.
