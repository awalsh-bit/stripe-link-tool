Project Manager Dashboard

A project is a job site: every open sales ticket shipping to the same address, added together, that clears the Project Parameters. Cards arrive on their own from the ePASS feed; the date is the earliest schedule date a salesperson put on a ticket for that site. Work the card: contact the site, visit, note what you find, get it ready for delivery.
