Promo Item Builder

Drag any unit in stock from the left onto a card. A model means any new unit of that model; a serial is one exact unit (open box, unique price). Everything about an item is ePASS's and stays read-only — the only thing you set is the price. Add the misc charges (delivery, install, recycle, promo installs) from the ePASS list so the total with tax is right. A card goes to the Secret Menu and/or the Online Shop when you publish it.
