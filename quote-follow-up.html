Quote Follow-Up

Open ePASS quotes that haven't turned into a sale yet, for Showroom and Field Sales Consultants. Quotes for the same client written within 30 days share one opportunity card. A finished order for the same customer number and salesperson (from the OE-23 feed) automatically moves the card to Converted; QUOTE 2s resolve themselves (converted with an order, lost without one) and never need work. For everything else, work the card: record a missing conversion, queue a follow-up, or mark the opportunity lost. The Scoreboard tab charts each consultant's mix of direct sales, conversions, and open pipeline for the date range.
