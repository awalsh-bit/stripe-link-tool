Repair Service Commissions

Every tech's weekly attainment — COD labor + COD parts margin + warranty labor (+ any quota credit) from finished SV tickets in the Sales Order Detail feed — against their weekly quota, and where the quarter's commission is trending: target × (weeks ÷ 52) × attainment% − base × (weeks ÷ 52), never below $0. Quarters run 12 / 14 / 12 / 14 fiscal weeks; the commission lands on the paycheck 20 days after the quarter's last Saturday.

Quota credits

Wilson quota credit — install fixes, client-sat visits, anything that should count toward a tech's week but isn't on a billable ticket.

Plans

One row per tech per year, straight from the Service Dept Quota tab: weekly quota $, target annual compensation, and the base-pay guarantee (blank = 60% of target). Tech code is the ePASS salesperson code on their SV tickets.
