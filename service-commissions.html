Repair Service Commissions

Every tech's weekly attainment — COD labor + COD parts margin + warranty labor (+ any quota credit) from finished SV tickets in the Sales Order Detail feed — against their weekly quota, and where the quarter's commission is trending: OTE × (weeks ÷ 52) × attainment% − base × (weeks ÷ 52), never below $0. Quarters run 12 / 14 / 12 / 14 fiscal weeks; the commission lands on the paycheck 20 days after the quarter's last Saturday.

Profitability estimate

Managers only. What the quarter is on track to bring in — labor + parts margin from finished SV tickets (quota credits excluded) — against what it costs: the base guarantee for the quarter (base × weeks ÷ 52), the trending commission, payroll burden on both, and fuel from each tech's card receipts. Fuel is informational — it never changes a commission. Projections carry the attainment % so far through the rest of the quarter.

Quota credits

Wilson quota credit — install fixes, client-sat visits, anything that should count toward a tech's week but isn't on a billable ticket.

Quarter plan

Set the department's quota for the quarter first, then split it among the techs. Each tech's weekly quota is their share of the dept total ÷ the quarter's weeks, so changing the dept number re-splits everyone on the spot. Techs populate from the employee directory by job title — add or remove titles below — and a tech can be added by code or taken off for one quarter. OTE is on-target earnings (the full annual pay at 100% of quota); the base guarantee is a percent of OTE (default 60%).
