Repair Service Commissions

Every tech's weekly attainment — COD labor + COD parts margin + warranty labor (+ any quota credit) from finished SV tickets in the Sales Order Detail feed — against their weekly quota, and where the quarter's commission is trending: OTE × (weeks ÷ 52) × attainment% − base × (weeks ÷ 52), never below $0. Quarters run 12 / 14 / 12 / 14 fiscal weeks; the commission lands on the paycheck 20 days after the quarter's last Saturday.

Profitability estimate

Managers only. What the quarter is on track to bring in — labor + parts margin from finished SV tickets (quota credits excluded) — against what it costs: the base guarantee for the quarter (base × weeks ÷ 52), the trending commission, payroll burden on both, and fuel from each tech's card receipts. Fuel is informational — it never changes a commission. Projections carry the attainment % so far through the rest of the quarter.

Quota credits

Wilson quota credit — install fixes, client-sat visits, anything that should count toward a tech's week but isn't on a billable ticket.

Quarter plan

Set the department's quota for the quarter first, then split it among the techs. Each tech's weekly quota is their share of the dept total ÷ the quarter's weeks, so changing the dept number re-splits everyone on the spot. Shares can add up to more than 100% — assigning techs above the dept target is the cushion that lets the department still hit its number when one tech misses. Techs populate from the employee directory by job title — add or remove titles below — and a tech can be added by code or taken off for one quarter. OTE is on-target earnings (the full annual pay at 100% of quota); the base guarantee is a percent of OTE (default 60%).

Qualifying job titles

Difficulty factors weight the split: each tech starts at an even share of the dept quota (1 ÷ techs on plan) and the gear (tech territory adjustments) sets Geo Mix, Geo Drive Time and Tenure, each a 5–25% swing of that even share — strictly for that tech, nobody else moves, so the total can run over 100% (the cushion). Weighted split sets everyone that way at once; changing a factor only re-sets that tech. Shares can still be nudged by hand afterward. Trailing 52 wks is the tech's average weekly quota and attainment over the last 52 completed fiscal weeks before this quarter (only weeks they had a plan) — the anchor for the new number, weighted to recency rather than season.
