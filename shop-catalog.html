Express Assortment — in-stock catalog

Every model the ePASS feed says is in stock and what the store does with it. The store posts every in-stock model automatically (new ALL-type units that nothing in ePASS has a claim on). 2–3 units show Low Stock, 4 or more show In Stock; a model with a single unit stays off until you turn on Show when only one. Price defaults to the model's RETAIL price in ePASS (the retail deck — MAP/PMAP), then a brand UMRP/MAP code, then L1; at or above MAP the price shows right on the card. Set an override to sharpen it (below MAP it shows in the cart only), or hide a model. Outlet units are the open-box and floor serials on the store — take a picture of one with your phone and that picture becomes its card on the shop, marked Actual product photo. Brands that don't allow e-commerce, delivery ZIPs and the same-day program live on Online Shop Orders.
