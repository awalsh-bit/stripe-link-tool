Express Assortment — in-stock catalog

Every model the ePASS feed says is in stock and what the store does with it. Each row shows the model's current setup — click a row to edit it: price override, show when only one, hide, chip, MAP exempt, and its pictures. The store posts in-stock models automatically: 2–3 units show Low Stock, 4 or more show In Stock, a single unit stays off until you turn on Show when only one. Price defaults to the model's RETAIL price in ePASS. Pictures you add replace the catalog thumbnail — Actual photos (ours; the shop marks them Actual product photo) and Supplier assets (the maker's own images), as many of either as you like; the first is the card picture. Outlet units are the open-box serials and any sealed unit with its own Unique Sell Price in ePASS; a phone picture of one becomes that unit's card, and Needs a price lists the ones ePASS hasn't priced yet. Brands that don't allow e-commerce, delivery ZIPs and the same-day program live on Online Shop Orders.
