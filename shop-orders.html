Same-day install program Cutoff and qualifying products for warehouse-team installs.

Promo codes Codes shoppers can apply in the cart — what they do and how many times they can be used.

A shopper types the code in the cart ("Have a promo code?") and the discount shows on the order summary, the checkout and the filed order below. Percent and dollar codes come off the appliances only (never parts, installation or delivery); Free delivery waives the White Glove fee. Sales tax is figured on the discounted amount. The redemption limit counts filed orders — canceling an order gives its redemption back. Leave the limit blank for unlimited; the per-customer limit defaults to once per shopper profile. Changes are live immediately.

Delivery ZIP codes Which ZIPs can enter the online shop and order.

A shopper has to enter one of these ZIPs to come in, and a profile's delivery address has to be in one to check out. Add several at once (commas or spaces); the label is optional (city, route…). Changes are live immediately. Removing a ZIP doesn't touch existing orders or profiles — it just stops new visitors and checkouts from that ZIP.
