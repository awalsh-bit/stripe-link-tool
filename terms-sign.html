Installation

Wilson AC & Appliance installs appliances in accordance with manufacturer guidelines for performance and safety reasons. Wilson AC & Appliances does not modify, or correct issues/problems with client property / other trades necessary to facilitate installation of appliances. These include but are not limited to framing, cabinetry, electrical supply, water supply, water drainage, gas supply, dryer vents, or hood ventilation systems. It is the responsibility of the contractor, project manager, or customer to ensure systems for the supply of gas, water, electrical, drainage, venting etc. meet local building codes, and are available to facilitate the installation using standard materials associated with the install of that appliance.

Units installed by Wilson AC & Appliance that are connected to water or gas will be checked at time of installation. If gas or water is NOT present at the time of installation and connections cannot be tested, Wilson AC & Appliance accepts no responsibility for leaks. Customer assumes liability for any water or gas leaks after completion of installation.

All pathways from the delivery truck to the final install location must allow for adequate clearance for safe movement of the installers, appliances, and client property. Make sure all areas from the truck to the home's entrance have a substantially wide hard-packed surface, the number of stairs are communicated to our installation dispatchers, and all doorways and hallways are measured appropriately. Additional fees may be assessed for difficult access that requires additional time, specialized equipment, or additional installers.

Wilson AC & Appliance provides standard basic flooring protection at no charge. Plywood, Masonite, or other means of protection are not included in our standard installation pricing. Contractors, project managers and customers are responsible for materials to protect floors. If requested, Wilson AC & Appliance can supply these materials at additional cost.

Remote ventilators, in-line ventilators, primary ducting for ventilation hoods is to be installed by respective trade professionals. Wilson AC & Appliance will provide minor ducting materials to connect to an existing duct system. Size & positioning of the duct system is crucial to allow for a properly sealed connection and to allow for satisfactory performance. If the installer does not have adequate access to connect to the duct system, installation cannot occur.

Sales

Special order appliance sales are final. Special order products include, but are not limited to, those that are ordered to custom specifications or in a specialty finish.

A 25% restocking fee will be assessed on any product returned after delivery / pickup and must be in the original or satisfactory packaging.

A 25% restocking fee will be assessed on any expanded assortment appliance cancelled after Wilson AC & Appliance accepts receipt of the product.

Open box models are sold as-is in terms of cosmetic appearance. Please inquire in regards to functional warranty for individual pieces as each manufacturer supports their products differently.

Pricing is subject to change in rare circumstances as required by manufacturer dealer agreements and / or significant delays in fulfillment.

Significant delays in the ability of the client to accept delivery of appliances after Wilson AC & Appliances accepts receipt of the inventory from the manufacturer may require repricing and reordering or the assessment of a monthly storage fee.

Payment is due in full prior to Wilson AC & Appliance accepting receipt of appliances from vendors. Credit accounts are subject to agreement terms.

Pickup orders must be completed within five days of scheduled pick up date. Orders not picked up within five days will be cancelled and refunded.
