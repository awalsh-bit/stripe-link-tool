Registration link

This is your own link: a registration that comes through it shows your name. It isn't posted anywhere, so a builder only has it when one of us sends it.

How it goes

  1. Walk the builder through the flyer, then send them your link.
  2. They register their company. You get a notification, and it shows under Registrations below.
  3. An executive approves or declines it here.
  4. You call or email the builder with the answer. Nothing is sent for you.
